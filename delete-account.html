How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your HURO account and all associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to shemaarnaud69@gmail.com with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your HURO account
  3. Include your full name for verification
  4. We will confirm your request within 3 business days
  5. Your account and data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of confirmation

What Happens After Deletion

  • You will be logged out of all devices immediately

  • You will no longer receive notifications from HURO
  • You can create a new account at any time after deletion
